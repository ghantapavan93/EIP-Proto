Most changes are effective October 1, 2026, and will require plans to update their marketing and agent oversight workflows well in advance of the annual enrollment period. The 48-hour waiting period between SOA completion and personal marketing appointments is eliminated. The 12-hour prohibition between educational and marketing events held at the same location is eliminated, provided beneficiaries are notified of the transition and given a meaningful opportunity to leave before the marketing event begins. SOA form collection at educational events is now expressly permitted. The prohibition on the use of superlatives in marketing materials without supporting documentation is removed, though the general prohibition on misleading or materially inaccurate communications remains in effect. The required retention period for marketing and sales call recordings is reduced from 10 to six years. The Notice of Availability of language assistance and auxiliary aids and services requirements are rescinded.
