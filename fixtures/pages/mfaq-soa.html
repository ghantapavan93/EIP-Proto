When and How to Complete Your Scope of Appointment in 2026 Timing matters with the SOA. As a general rule, the form must be completed *before* any personalized discussion of specific plan benefits, costs, or enrollment begins. For scheduled appointments, CMS guidelines traditionally suggest a 48-hour advance window - meaning the form should ideally be submitted at least two days before the meeting. However, the scope of appointment rules in 2026 include important flexibility. If you walk into an agent's office on your own initiative, or if you call to request a same-day appointment, the SOA can be completed the same day. This accommodates the reality that many beneficiaries make spontaneous decisions to get information and shouldn't face unnecessary delays. questions. Your Questions Answered: Medicare Scope of Appointment FAQs What exactly is a Medicare Scope of Appointment (SOA)? The SOA is a CMS-mandated form that authorizes an agent to discuss only the specific types of Medicare plans you select. It protects you from unsolicited sales pitches and keeps the conversation focused on your actual interests. When does an SOA need to be completed before a Medicare appointment? The SOA must be completed before any discussion of specific plan benefits or costs. For scheduled appointments, the standard expectation is 48 hours in advance, though same-day completion is permitted when you initiate the contact or walk in without a prior appointment. Can a Scope of Appointment be completed on the same day as my Medicare appointment? Yes. In 2026, CMS rules allow same-day SOA completion when you walk into an agent's office or initiate a same-day meeting yourself. This flexibility ensures you can get information when you need it without bureaucratic delays. What happens if an agent discusses plans not listed on my signed SOA? An agent is strictly prohibited from discussing any plan type not checked on your SOA. If this happens, you can end the appointment immediately and report the violation to CMS or the plan carrier. The agent's retained copy of the SOA serves as documentation. How long must agents retain Scope of Appointment forms? CMS requires agents and their agencies to retain completed SOA forms for a minimum of 10 years. This retention requirement creates accountability and ensures a paper trail exists if compliance questions arise later. How long is a Medicare Scope of Appointment good for? A Medicare Scope of Appointment is good for 12 months from the date it was signed. Within that 12-month window, the same SOA can be used for follow-up meetings between you and the same agent, as long as the discussion stays within the plan types originally checked on the form.
