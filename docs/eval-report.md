> were written by the author of the matchers; they catch regressions, not unknown unknowns.

Precision-first by design: a false positive costs a reviewer one click; a false negative is
covered by the LLM proposer and by the next human who reads the artifact. Cases whose ids end
in `near-miss` but still expect an edge are *documented known limitations* — the matcher fires
on context words the golden set deliberately stresses.

## Known limitations: real quotes no matcher covers

Kept in the suite so the gap stays visible. They expect no edge from today's rules, so
they do not move precision or recall; each says what would be needed to catch it.

- **r1-q01**: "Scope of appointment confirmation: written or recorded verbal agreement is required before discussing specific products"
  - Misdescribes the CY2027 changes by omission; no version-specific wording to bind. Needs a 'regulatory-description accuracy' rule (LLM proposal + human review), not a regex.
- **r1-q02**: "Recording and documentation requirements: certain calls must be recorded or logged for compliance review"
  - Mentions recording but no retention period, so it binds to neither v1 (10 years) nor v2 (6 years).
- **r1-q04**: "Our agents are contracted with every major carrier in Hillsborough County and can compare all 58 Medicare Advantage plans, Medigap policies, and Part D options at no cost to you."
  - Contradicts the page's own 'we do not offer every plan' disclaimer. Needs an 'all-plans claim vs disclaimer' rule.
- **r1-q07**: "The $2,000 annual Part D out-of-pocket cap continues in 2027, making formulary verification even more valuable for high-cost medications."
  - Wrong program parameter (the 2027 cap is $2,400). Needs a 'program parameters by plan year' rule.
- **r1-q08**: "Part D Maximum Out-of-Pocket $2,000 New cap effective 2025; includes the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan option"
  - Stale program parameter in a 2026 table. Needs a 'program parameters by plan year' rule.
- **r1-q09**: "Broad preferred pharmacy network. Gap coverage on generics."
  - The coverage gap ended in 2025. Needs a plan-year fact rule.
- **r1-q10**: "For 2026, the standard monthly premium is $202.90, up from $202.90 in 2025."
  - The 2025 Part B premium was $185. Needs a 'program parameters by plan year' rule.
- **r1-q14**: "Top-level contracts with the highest paid commissions Co-Op money"
  - Agent-facing compensation claim (FMV caps), not a beneficiary superlative. Needs a compensation rule and counsel.
- **r1-q15**: "Once your agency meets its goals, we automatically move you up to the next contract level, until you reach the top."
  - Production-tiered contract levels. No encoded rule; verify with counsel.
- **r1-q16**: "2024 AHIP Medicare Training AHIP Certification"
  - Certification two plan years stale. Needs a 'certification plan-year freshness' rule.
- **r1-q17**: "Our licensed Medicare agents provide free, unbiased guidance. We are compensated by insurance carriers, not by you."
  - 'Unbiased' from a commission-paid TPMO that does not offer every plan. No encoded misleading-claims rule.
- **r1-q18**: "Because CMS standardizes commission rates, a broker has no financial reason to steer you toward one carrier over another."
  - Overstates CMS commission rules (CMS sets a maximum, not a standard rate). agent-broker-compensation is now encoded, but its matchers look for CY2027 cap amounts and vacated administrative-payment language, not claims about what the caps mean.
- **r1-q19**: "Tampa (Hillsborough County) has 58 Medicare Advantage plans available in 2026, with 52 offering $0 monthly premiums and 74% rated 4 stars or higher by CMS"
  - Plan-year 2026 data on a page aimed at AEP 2027. Needs a 'plan-year freshness' rule.
- **r1-q20**: "Our licensed agents can review your current coverage, check your drug formulary, and help you find the best plan for 2027 at no cost to you."
  - Soft superlative in a service description. Deliberately not flagged: 'help you find the best plan' is excluded to keep false positives low (see sup-09-near-miss).
